# Privacy Policy for Cue

**Last updated: June 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Cue** ("we", "our", "the App") handles your information. 

## 1. No Collection of Personal Information
Cue is designed to run entirely locally on your device. 
* We **do not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII).
* All application configurations and settings are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to any remote servers.

## 2. Network Connections and Internet Access
Cue does not connect to external servers or transmit telemetry data. Any internet access is limited to:
* **Update Checks (if applicable):** The application may query the official GitHub repository or Microsoft Store to check for the latest versions of the software. No personal data is sent during this process.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App is published on the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may collect telemetry and usage statistics as governed by the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement). We do not have access to individual user telemetry from Microsoft, other than aggregated, anonymous installation and crash reports provided by the developer portal.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted by updating this file in our repository.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the repository page: [https://github.com/LyTblu7/Cue-App](https://github.com/LyTblu7/Cue-App).
